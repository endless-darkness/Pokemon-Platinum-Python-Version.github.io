About the game

Start in Twinleaf Town and work your way through Sinnoh’s towns, routes, and caves. You’ll meet rival trainers, challenge the Gyms, and run into Team Galactic along the way.

The game uses text, Pokémon sprites, and music. Menus show the keys you need, whether you’re choosing a move or sorting your PC boxes.

Things to do

  • Catch and evolve Pokémon, learn moves, and build a team.
  • Take on trainers and Gym Leaders in turn-based battles.
  • Enter Super Contests in Hearthome City.
  • Visit the Battle Zone and the Pokémon World Tournament.
